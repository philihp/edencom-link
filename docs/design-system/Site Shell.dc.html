1Site shell — sidebar nav, hamburger on mobile, new footer · dark and light
What replaces the header. The bracket nav was a flat row of five sections; it can't hold a growing app. Desktop moves to a persistent left rail: scope switcher at top (which corp/alt set you're looking at — the Supabase project picker equivalent), grouped sections in the middle, account pinned at the bottom. The rail collapses to icons at 64px. The bracket motif survives as the section-group label style, so it still reads as this product.
The ERP rethink. A classic ERP puts a hundred modules in a mega-menu and makes you know where things live. Here the rail carries only the eight nouns you actually navigate, and the ask bar (⌘K, pinned above the account block) covers the long tail — questions, jumps, and actions that would otherwise be buried menu leaves. Agent runs get a first-class rail item with a live count, because work now happens while you're away and needs somewhere to land.
Footer. No link farm. Desktop gets a one-line status bar welded to the bottom of the content pane — sync state, ESI health, build, sign-off. Mobile keeps the same line, stacked, under the content.
1aDesktop — rail expanded, assets section active. Status bar replaces the footer.
