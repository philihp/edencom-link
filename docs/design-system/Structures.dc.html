No ISK changed hands, so it is not revenue. change the rates » Taxes paid is facility tax we were actually charged, for jobs run in the structures listed above — ours and others' alike. Total EIV is the Estimated Item Value of our manufacturing and reaction jobs installed at these structures, priced from CCP's adjusted prices. The estimate assumes Omega installers; 37 jobs could not be priced (missing bill, price, or index history) and count toward nothing.
