1Industry — lift rate graph above the job list · dark and light
The measure. Each job carries a lift — Jita-split value of outputs minus Jita-split of inputs minus job fees, priced at install — spread evenly across its duration into an ISK/hr rate. The graph sums the rates of every job running at each instant, so it is a step function: it jumps when a job installs, drops when one delivers. Drawn as steps, not a smoothed curve, because that is what the data actually is.
Breakdown. A bracket toggle scopes the line to manufacturing or reactions; research is listed but inert until it can be measured. Idle slots are not counted — this is lift from jobs, not utilisation.
Digging back. Scroll over the chart to zoom about the cursor, shift-scroll or trackpad-swipe to pan; the floor is the oldest job on record. Hover shows the moment's rate and which jobs were running; scoping pins that moment and filters the list below to those jobs — the list header says so and offers a way back to active jobs.
